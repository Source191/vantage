# Proposal

## Why

`openspec\changes\archive` holds every finished change (about 515 KB across 37 changes). Sessions that browse it while orienting spend tokens on history that `openspec\specs` and CLAUDE.md already summarise, and may read superseded designs as current.

## What Changes

- CLAUDE.md's OpenSpec rule gains one sentence: the archive is history, searched only when asked or when checking why something was decided, never as part of orientation.
- No behaviour change, no spec change, no version bump: CLAUDE.md is not one of the files that require one.

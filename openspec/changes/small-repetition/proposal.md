# Proposal

## Why

The code review of 0.20.0-preview.7 (1 November 2026, sections 3.3, 3.7 and 3.13) left fourteen findings that are repetition or readability rather than behaviour: a crash line kept by matching text another class writes, "the tabs owned by this session" spelled four times, five catches that turn failures into blanks with no diagnostic, palette colours picked three ways, two pull-request adapters sharing 35 lines, three copies of one escape-sequence grammar, and a couple of dozen comment, naming and layout nits. None earns a change of its own; together they are a day's work in a few commits, and each one left is a trap for the next reader. Written against `main` ac7cdae0 and re-read against 8dbf4150 (0.20.0-preview.13), after PRs #175 to #180 landed.

## What Changes

- **Domain:** `TabRegistrations.For(SessionId)` answers the registrations a session owns, replacing the four hand-written filters in `OpenTabHandler.cs:36, 58`, `RenameSessionHandler.cs:25` and `SessionTabs.cs:16` (A-11). `TabPalette` names its colours (`Green`, `Yellow`, the last one as `Last`), so `OwnTab.cs:12`, `TabScripts.cs:12, 15` and `TabScriptsShould.cs:43, 55` pick by intent rather than by `Name == "Green"`, `[2]` and `[^1]` (A-23). `SessionId.cs:14` loses the guard `IsSessionId` already covers, `BranchName.cs:5` says its rule is Vantage's minimal one (branch text comes from slugs and git's own output), and `WhereLabel.cs:92-93` loses its double blank line (D-17).
- **Diagnostics:** `DiagnosticLine.IsCrashLine(string)` owns the crash-line test, so `ProblemReports.cs:19` stops matching `" failed Crash: "` by hand (which misses a crash with a `What`, rendered `failed Crash <what>:` by the `Failed` arm at `DiagnosticLine.cs:57`), and the Core.Tests fixtures derive the line from `DiagnosticLine` or assert `IsCrashLine` instead of spelling it (A-09). The five swallowing catches in `WorktreeInventory.cs:106, 152`, `SetupFactsReader.cs:32` (one catch guarding three reads) and `Detail\DetailLoader.cs:60, 72` record a `Failed(FailureSite.Load, ...)` through `Attempt`, once per folder per run for the scan and the detail reads so the 500-event log stays quiet (A-19). `RecordedProcessRunner.Started<T>` (`:39`) becomes `GuardingStart<T>`: since PR #179 the `Started` event is a record in the same namespace (`Diagnostics\Started.cs:6`), so the method now shares a type's name as well as an event's (A-22).
- **Application tidies:** `StartUpdateHandler` takes the `VantageProcess` that `Application\Effects\Composition.cs:38` already unpacks; `FolderLabels` takes `CurrentSettings` and computes the roots-plus-worktree-root list itself, with a `FolderLabelsShould` fact, so the rule leaves the untested lambda at `Composition\ConsoleComposition.cs:50` (A-21: PR #177 moved `Program.cs`'s other decisions into `ConsoleLoads`, `HeaderSettingsRow`, `UpdateCheckSwitch` and `RequiredPrograms`; these two lines are what it left). `Wording.ConfigBar` and `WorktreesBar` (`WordingOfConsole.cs:33, 113`) drop the `state` parameter they guard and never read (A-22). `WorktreeInventory.cs:33-44`'s else-if chain becomes a `FolderKind` enum and a switch, `SessionRowEnricher.cs:35`'s `AddRange` of a zero-or-one collection becomes an `if`, and `:17`'s fully qualified `ConcurrentDictionary` becomes a `using` (A-24). `RowDecision.cs:9` stops describing a "still loading" case, `SettingsScope.cs:27` drops `row?.` on a non-nullable parameter and `ConfigView.cs:92` builds `FolderPath.From(path)` once (V-12).
- **Adapters:** `GitHubPullRequests` and `AzureDevOpsPullRequests` share `CliListing.RunAsync` (the `Win32Exception` to `CliMissing` shell at `GitHubPullRequests.cs:31-39` and `AzureDevOpsPullRequests.cs:37-45`) and `PullRequestListingJson.Rows` (the parse and its catch at `:50-70` and `:57-77`); the shared parser decides once that a row missing a property is skipped, as a non-https row already is, rather than failing the whole listing (I-08, see design.md). `Desktop.StartAsync`'s summary says it is synchronous (I-16, narrowed: see "Already fixed"). `GitWorktreeListing.cs:53-54` loses `default: break;` and `GitWorktreeListingShould` pins the empty-path entry a block without a `worktree` line gives (I-17). `ClaudeSettings.Serialise` appends the newline as a byte instead of decoding and re-encoding the document (I-18).
- **Tui:** `IScreenDefinition.cs:39` stops saying "such as "Sessions (6)""; `ConsoleScreen.cs:51`'s `shown` moves into `Frame`, where the one run lives; `WizardControls.cs:160-173`'s `Handled` and `Button` move to a `DialogButtons` static that `LaunchWizardDialog`, `SettingsDialog` and `ProblemReportDialog` call (V-12).
- **Tools:** one ECMA-48 hand scanner, `EscapeSequences.EndOf`, replaces the two `Csi` regexes (`EscapeSequences.cs:13`, `CaptureMarker.cs:80`) and `ScreenReplay`'s own scanner (`ScreenReplay.cs:91-125, 192`), so Vantage's DECAC reset `ESC [ 2;263;264 , |` (`TabEscapes.cs:20`, an intermediate `,` and a final `|`) is stripped rather than left in the plain text or scanned past; `CaptureMarker.TitleSequence` stays the one regex, and `TerminalQueries.cs:15-27`'s six run-time regexes become `[GeneratedRegex]` methods (T-07, T-19).
- **Layout:** `.editorconfig` gains `dotnet_style_allow_multiple_blank_lines_experimental = false` and IDE2000 at warning, and every double blank line under `src`, `test` and `tools` goes with it (re-scanned on 8dbf4150: eight places under `src`, one of them a triple at `WorktreeRow.cs:31-33`, sixteen under `test`, none under `tools`); the missing blank lines before a doc comment (`ListSelection.cs:34-35`) and before `namespace` (`GitWorktreeListing.cs:3`) go by hand (A-24, V-12).
- **Already fixed on `main`, dropped:** `StopSessionHandler` has its summary and `Detail\DetailLoader.cs:25-27`'s `<param>` tags are filled (A-22); `InheritedEnvironment.cs:5` names `GIT_TERMINAL_PROMPT` and why (D-17); `TableBody.cs:388` has one `ScrollStep` summary (V-12); PR #178 (editor-not-found) gave `Desktop`'s class summary `OpenAsync`'s contract (the PowerShell child is waited for, so a missing file is reported) and `StartAsync` its own summary and `ProgramNotStartedException`, with both stated on `IDesktop` too, so of I-16 only the synchronous-start sentence is left.
- **Left out on purpose:** A-19's "counts unavailable" marker in place of zeros when git cannot read a worktree: `WordingOfDetail.cs:61` and `WordingOfConsole.cs:75-76` show those counts, so it would change what the detail pane and the counts say and belongs to a spec'd change. V-12's reducer nits (the `d` arms in `SessionsView`, the parentheses in `WorktreesView`, the standing-message comments) belong to the reducer change.
- **Version:** one preview step above `main` (0.20.0-preview.14 if nothing lands first), because files under `src\` change; patch-level under semantic versioning. One line under Unreleased in `CHANGELOG.md`, under Internal, which `Assert-ChangelogUpdated.ps1` requires since PR #180.
- `skip_specs: true`: no key, screen, message, state file or command line behaves differently. The one edge that moves, a pull-request row missing a property, is covered by no requirement in `openspec\specs\pull-requests\spec.md` and is flagged in design.md for the owner.

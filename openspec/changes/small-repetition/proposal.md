# Proposal

## Why

The code review of 0.20.0-preview.7 (1 November 2026, sections 3.3, 3.7 and 3.13) left fourteen findings that are repetition or readability rather than behaviour: a crash line kept by matching text another class writes, "the tabs owned by this session" spelled four times, five catches that turn failures into blanks with no diagnostic, palette colours picked three ways, two pull-request adapters sharing 35 lines, three copies of one escape-sequence grammar, and a couple of dozen comment, naming and layout nits. None earns a change of its own; together they are a day's work in a few commits, and each one left is a trap for the next reader.

## What Changes

- **Domain:** `TabRegistrations.For(SessionId)` answers the registrations a session owns, replacing the four hand-written filters in `OpenTabHandler.cs:36, 58`, `RenameSessionHandler.cs:25` and `SessionTabs.cs:16` (A-11). `TabPalette` names its colours (`Green`, the last one as `Last`), so `OwnTab.cs:12`, `TabScripts.cs:12, 15` and `TabScriptsShould.cs:43, 55` pick by intent rather than by `Name == "Green"`, `[2]` and `[^1]` (A-23). `SessionId.cs:14` loses the guard `IsSessionId` already covers, `BranchName.cs:5` says its rule is Vantage's minimal one (branch text comes from slugs and git's own output), and `WhereLabel.cs:92-93` loses its double blank line (D-17).
- **Diagnostics:** `DiagnosticLine.IsCrashLine(string)` owns the crash-line test, so `ProblemReports.cs:19` stops matching `" failed Crash: "` by hand (which misses a crash with a `What`, rendered `failed Crash <what>:`), and the Core.Tests fixtures derive the line from `DiagnosticLine` or assert `IsCrashLine` instead of spelling it (A-09). The five swallowing catches in `WorktreeInventory.cs:106, 152`, `SetupFactsReader.cs:32` (one catch guarding three reads) and `DetailLoader.cs:60, 72` record a `Failed(FailureSite.Load, ...)` through `Attempt`, once per folder per run for the scan and the detail reads so the 500-event log stays quiet (A-19). `RecordedProcessRunner.Started<T>` becomes `GuardingStart<T>`, no longer sharing the `Started` event's name (A-22).
- **Application tidies:** `StartUpdateHandler` takes the `VantageProcess` that `Composition.cs:37` already holds; `FolderLabels` takes `CurrentSettings` and computes the roots-plus-worktree-root list itself, with a `FolderLabelsShould` fact, so the rule leaves `Program.cs:153` (A-21). `Wording.ConfigBar` and `WorktreesBar` drop the `state` parameter they guard and never read (A-22). `WorktreeInventory.cs:33-44`'s else-if chain becomes a `FolderKind` enum and a switch, `SessionRowEnricher.cs:35`'s `AddRange` of a zero-or-one collection becomes an `if`, and `:17`'s fully qualified `ConcurrentDictionary` becomes a `using` (A-24). `RowDecision.cs:9` stops describing a "still loading" case, `SettingsScope.cs:27` drops `row?.` on a non-nullable parameter and `ConfigView.cs:92` builds `FolderPath.From(path)` once (V-12).
- **Adapters:** `GitHubPullRequests` and `AzureDevOpsPullRequests` share `CliListing.RunAsync` (the `Win32Exception` to `CliMissing` shell) and `PullRequestListingJson.Rows` (the parse and its catch); the shared parser decides once that a row missing a property is skipped, as a non-https row already is, rather than failing the whole listing (I-08, see design.md). `Desktop`'s class summary states both methods' contracts (I-16). `GitWorktreeListing.cs:53-54` loses `default: break;` and `GitWorktreeListingShould` pins the empty-path entry a block without a `worktree` line gives (I-17). `ClaudeSettings.Serialise` appends the newline as a byte instead of decoding and re-encoding the document (I-18).
- **Tui:** `IScreenDefinition.cs:39` stops saying "such as Sessions (6)"; `ConsoleScreen.cs:51`'s `shown` moves into `Frame`, where the one run lives; `WizardControls.cs:160-173`'s `Handled` and `Button` move to a `DialogButtons` static that `LaunchWizardDialog`, `SettingsDialog` and `ProblemReportDialog` call (V-12).
- **Tools:** one ECMA-48 hand scanner, `EscapeSequences.EndOf`, replaces the two `Csi` regexes and `ScreenReplay`'s own scanner, so Vantage's DECAC reset `ESC [ 2;263;264 , |` (`TabEscapes.cs:20`, an intermediate `,` and a final `|`) is stripped rather than left in the plain text or scanned past; `CaptureMarker.TitleSequence` stays the one regex, and `TerminalQueries.cs:17-27`'s six run-time regexes become `[GeneratedRegex]` methods (T-07, T-19).
- **Layout:** `.editorconfig` gains `dotnet_style_allow_multiple_blank_lines_experimental = false` and IDE2000 at warning, and every double blank line under `src`, `test` and `tools` goes with it; the missing blank lines before a doc comment (`ListSelection.cs:34`, `WorktreeRow.cs:31-33` and the three screen definitions' `RowTip`) go by hand (A-24, V-12).
- **Already fixed on `main`, dropped:** `StopSessionHandler` has its summary and `DetailLoader.cs:25-27`'s `<param>` tags are filled (A-22); `InheritedEnvironment.cs:5` names `GIT_TERMINAL_PROMPT` and why (D-17); `TableBody.cs:388` has one `ScrollStep` summary (V-12).
- **Left out on purpose:** A-19's "counts unavailable" marker in place of zeros when git cannot read a worktree: `WordingOfDetail.cs:61` and `WordingOfConsole.cs:75-76` show those counts, so it would change what the detail pane and the counts say and belongs to a spec'd change. V-12's reducer nits (the `d` arms in `SessionsView`, the parentheses in `WorktreesView`, the standing-message comments) belong to the reducer change.
- **Version:** the next preview above `main` (0.20.0-preview.9 if nothing lands first), because files under `src\` change; patch-level under semantic versioning.
- `skip_specs: true`: no key, screen, message, state file or command line behaves differently. The one edge that moves, a pull-request row missing a property, is covered by no requirement in `openspec\specs\pull-requests\spec.md` and is flagged in design.md for the owner.

## Impact

- **Code:** Domain (`TabRegistrations`, `TabPalette`, `SessionId`, `BranchName`, `WhereLabel`); Application (`DiagnosticLine`, `ProblemReports`, `Attempt`, a new `Diagnostics\OncePerFolder`, `RecordedProcessRunner`, `WorktreeInventory`, `SetupFactsReader`, `DetailLoader`, `OpenTabHandler`, `RenameSessionHandler`, `SessionTabs`, `StartUpdateHandler`, `Composition`, `FolderLabels`, `WordingOfConsole`, `OwnTab`, `TabScripts`, `SessionRowEnricher`, `RowDecision`, `SettingsScope`, `ConfigView`); Adapters (the two pull-request adapters and two new helpers beside them, `Desktop`, `GitWorktreeListing`, `ClaudeSettings`); Tui (`IScreenDefinition`, `ConsoleScreen`, `WizardControls`, a new `DialogButtons`, the three dialogs, `SessionsScreenDefinition`, `WorktreesScreenDefinition`, `ConfigScreenDefinition`); tools (`EscapeSequences`, `CaptureMarker`, `ScreenReplay`, `TerminalQueries`); `Program.cs:145, 153, 154, 158` for the three constructors that gain a `DiagnosticLog` and `FolderLabels`; `.editorconfig`.
- **Tests:** new `TabRegistrationsShould`, `OncePerFolderShould`, `EscapeSequencesShould`; new facts in `TabPaletteShould`, `DiagnosticLineShould`, `AttemptShould`, `FolderLabelsShould`, `GitWorktreeListingShould`, `GitHubPullRequestsShould`, `AzureDevOpsPullRequestsShould`, `ScreenReplayShould`, `WorktreeInventoryLeftoversShould`, `DetailLoaderShould`, `SetupFactsReaderShould`; updated fixtures in `ProblemReportsShould`, `ProblemReportShould`, `ProblemReportHandlersShould`, `DiagnosticsPrivacyShould`, `TabScriptsShould`, `WordingOfKeyBarShould`, `StartUpdateHandlerShould`, `WorktreeInventoryShould`; `tools\HeadlessTerminal.Tests` gains a reference to the Infrastructure project so one fact reads `TabEscapes.ResetBackground`, recorded in its `README.md`.
- **Other open changes:** `tidy-composition-root` moves `Program.cs`'s construction into `ConsoleComposition`, so it lands first and this change edits the constructor calls there; `performance-diagnostics` (`DiagnosticLine`, `WorktreeInventory`), `table-body-collaborators` (`ConsoleScreen`), `wording-one-home` (`WordingOfConsole`), `columns-as-data` (`IScreenDefinition`) and `test-hygiene` (blank lines in tests) touch the same files: whichever lands second rebases, and every edit here is small enough to re-apply.
- **Users:** none; the diagnostic log and a problem report gain `failed Load ...` lines when a scan or a detail read fails.

## Out of scope

- A health-neutral marker for counts git could not read (A-19's second half): a spec'd change.
- The reducer and state reshaping of V-10 and the rest of V-12, `TableBody`'s collaborators (V-09), `IScreenDefinition`'s members (V-08) and `Wording`'s own repetition (A-12, A-13).
- Any change to what the pull-request listings ask of `gh` and `az`, or to the words they read.
- Verifying at the terminal that Windows Terminal honours the DECAC reset as written.
- Multiple Claude accounts, which is a later change.
